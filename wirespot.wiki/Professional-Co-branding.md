# 🏢 Professional Co-branding

## Overview

Professional users can configure their own business identity in WireSpot. This branding appears on customer-facing materials like voucher receipts and reports, while WireSpot remains identified as **powered by Vexel Innovations in collaboration with TechNova Technologies**.

---

## Configurable Fields

| Field | Used In |
|-------|---------|
| **Business name** | Receipts, reports |
| **Business email** | Receipts, reports |
| **Business phone** | Receipts, reports |
| **Business website** | Receipts, reports |
| **Business logo** | Receipts (raster print), reports |

---

## Setting Up Co-branding

1. Open **Settings** → **Business branding and logo**.
2. Enter your business name, email, phone, and website.
3. Tap **Choose logo** to select a PNG or JPG from the phone gallery.
4. Tap **Save co-branding**.

WireSpot copies the selected image into app storage so receipts and reports can reuse it reliably.

---

## Where Branding Appears

### Voucher Receipts
- Operator business name as the receipt header
- Operator contact details
- Operator logo (if set) as a raster image before receipt text
- WireSpot powered-by footer

### Reports
- Operator business identity in the PDF/CSV header
- Vexel Innovations collaboration line

### Share Output
- Shared receipt text includes operator details
