# 📊 Reports and Export

## Overview

WireSpot tracks voucher sales and revenue, providing summaries and export capabilities for operator record-keeping.

---

## Report Types

| Report | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Daily** | Revenue and sales for the current day |
| **Weekly** | Aggregated 7-day summary |
| **Monthly** | Aggregated 30-day summary |

Each report includes:
- 💰 Revenue total
- 🎫 Sales count
- 📋 Voucher history summaries

---

## Export Formats

### CSV Export

- Detailed sales data in CSV format
- Includes minor-unit and major-unit amounts
- Ready for spreadsheet analysis

### PDF-Text Export

- Branded summary with Vexel Innovations and TechNova Technologies collaboration line
- Detailed sales sections
- Professional layout for printing or archiving

---

## Sharing

Reports are shared through the **Android share sheet**, so you can:

- 📧 Email reports
- 💬 Send via WhatsApp or messaging apps
- 📁 Save to Google Drive or local storage
- 🖨️ Print through the Android print framework

---

## Co-branding on Reports

If [[Professional Co-branding]] is configured, reports will include:

- Operator business name
- Operator contact details
- WireSpot powered-by notice

---

## How to Generate a Report

1. Open **Reports** from the Dashboard.
2. Select the time range: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
3. Review the revenue summary and sales history.
4. Tap **Export CSV** or **Export PDF** to generate the file.
5. Use the **Share** action to send or save the report.
